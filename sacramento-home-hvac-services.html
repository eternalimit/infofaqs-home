Sacramento Home HVAC Services: Homeowner FAQs

Heating and cooling decisions depend on your home's equipment, comfort needs, energy use, and local requirements. These questions are a starting point for Sacramento-area homeowners, not a substitute for a licensed professional's assessment.

1. When should I request HVAC repair?

Seek a qualified technician when heating or cooling stops working, airflow becomes weak, unusual sounds or odors appear, or the system repeatedly cycles on and off. For a suspected gas leak, leave the area and contact your utility or emergency services rather than troubleshooting.

2. Repair or replace?

Compare the diagnosis and written repair estimate against the system's age, efficiency, reliability, and the installed price of replacement. Ask contractors to explain the tradeoffs before deciding.

3. What about heat pumps?

Heat pumps can provide both heating and cooling. Whether one is suitable depends on the building, electrical capacity, ductwork, and equipment selection.

4. Does system sizing matter?

Yes. An appropriately sized system should be based on the home's heating and cooling loads rather than square footage alone. Ask about the contractor's sizing method.

5. What maintenance should I plan?

Check filters according to manufacturer guidance, keep supply and return vents unobstructed, and schedule qualified service as appropriate for the equipment.

6. Are permits or energy-code checks required?

Requirements depend on the scope and jurisdiction. Ask the contractor and local building department about permits, California Title 24 energy standards, and applicable testing.

7. Are rebates available?

Programs and eligibility change. Check SMUD's official website for current energy-efficiency offerings before relying on a rebate estimate.

9. What should I ask before hiring?

Request licensing and insurance details, a written scope and price, warranty terms, permit responsibility, expected schedule, and a clear explanation of the recommended system.
